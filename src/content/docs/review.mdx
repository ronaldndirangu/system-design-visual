Practice recalling ideas to help them stick. This queue brings back questions from completed visual concept checks,
lesson quizzes and checkpoints. Missed ideas enter the queue immediately; correct answers first return tomorrow.

Each question moves through four boxes. A right answer moves it up one box, so it waits longer before it returns: 1 day,
then 3, then 7, then 21. A wrong answer sends it back to the first box, and you see it again tomorrow. A session holds
up to 20 questions, so a daily review takes about 10 minutes.

Your schedule lives in this browser alongside your concept and lesson progress. Nothing leaves your device.
