You forget most of a lesson within a week unless you pull it back out of memory. This queue brings back questions from
the lessons and checkpoints you have finished, spaced so each review lands just before you would forget.

Each question moves through four boxes. A right answer moves it up one box, so it waits longer before it returns: 1 day,
then 3, then 7, then 21. A wrong answer sends it back to the first box, and you see it again tomorrow. A session holds
up to 20 questions, so a daily review takes about 10 minutes.

Your schedule lives in this browser alongside your lesson progress. Nothing leaves your device.
